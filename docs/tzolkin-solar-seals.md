# Tzolkin Solar Seals and Maya Day Signs

## Purpose

This document defines the 20-sign information that Chrono Compass may display in the future Tzolkin calendar UI.

Chrono Compass uses Dreamspell Solar Seals as one interpretive layer, while also preserving the corresponding Maya day-sign names and meanings as a separate layer.

These systems are historically related through the shared 20-position sacred-calendar cycle, but Dreamspell names such as **Dragon**, **Worldbridger**, **Skywalker**, and **Wizard** are modern Dreamspell names and must not be presented as literal translations of all traditional Maya names.

The UI should eventually allow users to show or hide these layers independently.

## Living Maya meaning summaries

The following summaries are based primarily on material written by the K’iche’ Day Keepers group Komon Tohil of Zunil, Guatemala, published by the Smithsonian National Museum of the American Indian. They describe one living K’iche’ tradition and should not be presented as exhaustive definitions for all Maya peoples, places, or historical periods.

### 1 — Imox / Imix’ — Dreamspell Dragon

Associated with sea, rivers, lakes and rain; a nawal of water. The day is used for petitions concerning return, weather, home problems, and mental or spiritual disturbance. Its themes include intuition, creativity, water, and the deep or unsettled forces of life.

**Dreamspell:** Birth / Nurtures / Being.

### 2 — Iq’ / Ik’ — Dreamspell Wind

Wind, air, spirit, and the “heart of the sky.” It can include both life-giving breath and powerful weather such as storms or hurricanes. The day is associated with communication, emotional life, relief from suffering, and the movement of spirit.

**Dreamspell:** Spirit / Communicates / Breath.

### 3 — Aq’ab’al / Ak’b’al — Dreamspell Night

Darkness and dawn together: the threshold where night becomes light. It is associated with beginnings, illumination, the hand, and feelings of the heart.

**Dreamspell:** Abundance / Dreams / Intuition.

### 4 — K’at / K’an — Dreamspell Seed

Net, entanglement and disentanglement; also fire and protective or fishing nets. It can symbolize bonds, constraints, what catches or contains, and the work of freeing what has become tangled.

**Dreamspell:** Flowering / Targets / Awareness.

### 5 — Kan / Chikchan — Dreamspell Serpent

The feathered serpent; associated with the shaping of the universe, justice, truth, peace, strength, health, and work. It carries strong life-force and transformative imagery.

**Dreamspell:** Life Force / Survives / Instinct.

### 6 — Kame / Kimi — Dreamspell Worldbridger

Death, including the necessary relation between ending and continuation. In living practice it is connected with protection from harmful choices and accidents and with the presence of mortality in the cycle of life.

**Dreamspell:** Death / Equalizes / Opportunity.

### 7 — Kej / Manik’ — Dreamspell Hand

Deer. It is associated with the four supports or four corners of the world and with strength that carries human destiny. It has strong themes of nature, guardianship, balance, and support.

**Dreamspell:** Accomplishment / Knows / Healing.

### 8 — Q’anil / Lamat — Dreamspell Star

Seed and yellow/golden color. It is associated with fertility, plants, animals, people, seasonal cycles, growth, and the potential carried within a seed.

**Dreamspell:** Elegance / Beautifies / Art.

### 9 — Toj / Muluk — Dreamspell Moon

Offering, payment, fine, help, listening, and understanding. It is strongly associated with reciprocity, obligation, fire, restoring balance, and bringing what is hidden into the light.

**Dreamspell:** Universal Water / Purifies / Flow.

### 10 — Tz’i’ / Ok — Dreamspell Dog

Dog. Associated with the five human senses and with material and spiritual justice, judgment, law, protection, and avoiding destructive habits or misfortune.

**Dreamspell:** Heart / Loves / Loyalty.

### 11 — B’atz’ / Chuwen — Dreamspell Monkey

Thread. It symbolizes the thread of time, destiny, continuity with the past, and the development of humanity and nature. In contemporary K’iche’ practice it is especially important for the 260-day cycle and the initiation of new day keepers.

**Dreamspell:** Magic / Plays / Illusion.

### 12 — E / Eb’ — Dreamspell Human

Path or road. Associated with travel, destiny as a path, well-being, relationship, and communication with ancestors.

**Dreamspell:** Free Will / Influences / Wisdom.

### 13 — Aj / B’en — Dreamspell Skywalker

Cane, cornfield, and maize. It is associated with the upright stalk or staff, virtue, authority, home, cultivated life, and triumph.

**Dreamspell:** Space / Explores / Wakefulness.

### 14 — I’x / Ix — Dreamspell Wizard

Jaguar or wild feline imagery; vitality, sacred altars, Earth, wisdom, and the sacred presence of nature. It is a powerful day connected with the living Earth and spiritual force.

**Dreamspell:** Timelessness / Enchants / Receptivity.

### 15 — Tz’ikin / Men — Dreamspell Eagle

Bird. Associated with what moves through the sky and space, vision, fortune, trade, resources, and opportunity. It is traditionally important for prosperity and business.

**Dreamspell:** Vision / Creates / Mind.

### 16 — Ajmaq / K’ib’ — Dreamspell Warrior

Associated with will, mistakes, responsibility, forgiveness, and the recognition or correction of error. It is a day for reflection on conduct and restoring well-being.

**Dreamspell:** Intelligence / Questions / Fearlessness.

### 17 — No’j / Kab’an — Dreamspell Earth

Wisdom, reason, thought, judgment, and knowledge. It is associated with joining ideas, advice, science, learning, and the disciplined use of mind.

**Dreamspell:** Navigation / Evolves / Synchronicity.

### 18 — Tijax / Etz’nab’ — Dreamspell Mirror

Obsidian knife, destiny, cutting, and protection. It is associated with separation, surgery or healing, sharp judgment, removing harmful influences, and the danger as well as usefulness of a cutting edge.

**Dreamspell:** Endlessness / Reflects / Order.

### 19 — Kawoq / Kawak — Dreamspell Storm

Thunder, community and collective life; the Smithsonian source also records associations with ants and woman. It is a day for communal projects, prosperity, healing, and the welfare of the group.

**Dreamspell:** Self-Generation / Catalyzes / Energy.

### 20 — Ajpu’ / Ajaw — Dreamspell Sun

Lord Sun; life, destiny, plants, animals, hunter, marksman and walker. It carries solar authority, completion, clarity, talent, and the fulfillment of communal intention.

**Dreamspell:** Universal Fire / Enlightens / Life.

## Important distinction

The Maya day signs and Dreamspell Solar Seals occupy the same 20-position cycle, but their names and interpretive systems are not interchangeable.

Examples:

- Dreamspell **Dragon** corresponds structurally to Imox / Imix’, but “Dragon” is not a literal translation of Imox.
- Dreamspell **Hand** occupies the Kej / Manik’ position, whose living K’iche’ meaning centers strongly on deer and the four supports of the world.
- Dreamspell **Star** occupies Q’anil / Lamat, while Q’anil is strongly associated with seed, fertility and growth.
- Dreamspell **Wizard** occupies I’x / Ix, whose Maya imagery includes jaguar, Earth and sacred power.
- Dreamspell **Warrior** occupies Ajmaq / K’ib’, whose living K’iche’ interpretation includes responsibility, error and forgiveness.

Chrono Compass should make this distinction visible instead of silently replacing one tradition with another.

## UI intent

Seal/day-sign information should be modular. A user may eventually choose to display any combination of:

- Dreamspell color and Solar Seal name;
- Dreamspell Power / Action / Essence;
- Dreamspell seal glyph/artwork;
- Maya day-sign name;
- language-specific Maya name, such as K’iche’ and/or Yucatec;
- traditional/living Maya meaning summary;
- traditional Maya glyph where an appropriate sourced asset is available;
- Space Cross / Heavenly Order relationship information.

These options are informational layers only. Hiding an interpretation must not alter the underlying 20-sign sequence or Kin calculation.

## Sources and cautions

Traditional meanings differ between Maya languages, communities, historical periods, and lineages. Chrono Compass should therefore label the origin of displayed traditional material and avoid presenting one contemporary interpretation as the single universal ancient meaning.

The Smithsonian source used here explicitly credits the K’iche’ Day Keepers group **Komon Tohil**, Zunil, Quetzaltenango, Guatemala.
