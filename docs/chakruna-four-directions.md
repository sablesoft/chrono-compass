# Chakruna: Four Directions

## Purpose

This document records the **Chakruna** interpretive layer used by Chrono Compass for the four directions.

Chakruna is not presented as Dreamspell doctrine, as a universal historical Maya teaching, or as a scientific model. It is a distinct interpretive system in which the four cardinal directions are treated as living qualities of manifested reality. Across cultures, myths, symbols, psychological models and ritual systems, different traditions may be understood as partial maps of these same four underlying directional qualities.

The working premise of Chakruna is that the four directions are not merely labels placed on a diagram. They are manifestations of deeper qualities that recur through nature, perception, psychology, myth and cyclic process. Human cultures can therefore be read as accumulating partial maps of the same fourfold reality, each tradition discovering some correspondences and introducing its own language and distortions.

Chrono Compass may gradually expose Chakruna as an optional interpretive layer. Its terminology and symbolism must remain clearly attributed to Chakruna so that it is not confused with Dreamspell, living Maya traditions, historical source material or astronomical calculation.

The number **4** is central to Chakruna. The number 13 is important elsewhere in Chrono Compass, especially for Waves, but it is not the primary organizing number of Chakruna.

## Core fourfold map

| Stage | Direction | Color | Element | Process | Consciousness / psyche | Archetypal identity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | East | Red | Earth | Initiation / manifestation | Conscious mind | Ego |
| 2 | North | White | Air | Purification / effort / ascent | Awareness | Spirit |
| 3 | West | Blue | Water | Transformation / crisis / encounter with mystery | Subconscious | Soul |
| 4 | South | Yellow / Gold | Creative Fire | Completion / integration / evolution | Unconscious integrated into superconscious potential | Divine spark |

These rows are not independent labels. Each direction is understood as a deep qualitative field that can manifest through many different images, myths, psychological states and stages of process.

A compact cyclic reading is:

```text
South -> East -> North -> West -> South
latent whole -> manifestation -> ascent/clarity -> transformation/mystery -> integrated whole
seed -> sprout -> tree/axis -> crisis/unknown -> seed
```

The same cycle can be entered from East when describing a manifested process, but South is the hidden source and completion point from which the next manifested cycle can arise.

## East — Earth — Red

East is the first manifestation of a new cycle.

Its primary image is the **sprout**: something hidden in the seed begins to appear in form. East includes the initial idea, desire, intention, plan, model, image, goal or first impulse from which a process can begin.

The red color is associated with **clay and living earth**: matter that can receive form.

Psychologically, East corresponds to the **conscious mind** and, in a simple archetypal formulation, to the **ego**. It is the human world of descriptions, explanations, names, concepts and ordinary reality as it is consciously organized.

Understanding, in the conceptual sense, belongs strongly to East. One can describe a process, imagine it or build a model of it before one has acquired embodied knowledge of it.

In the plant image:

- South is the seed;
- East is the sprout emerging from it.

The East therefore gives visible structure to what South held in latent form.

## North — Air — White

North is the phase of ascent, effort, purification and connection.

Its major archetype is the **World Axis / Tree of Life**: the vertical structure connecting levels of reality. The sprout of East becomes trunk, branches and crown; growth now requires effort, direction and the ability to pass through resistance.

Air is the moving and mediating element. It crosses distance, links what is separate and represents the work required to move beyond the initial conscious formulation.

Psychologically, North corresponds to **awareness** rather than merely conscious description. It is the path of disciplined attention, concentration, refinement and spiritual effort. In the simple archetypal formulation, North corresponds to **Spirit**.

North is also associated with **clarity**. In the Chakruna process model, clarity can become a trap if it is mistaken for completion: a person may become attached to spiritual certainty, purity or luminous explanation and stop moving further into the unknown.

In cyclic geometry, North often appears as a state of **maximum extension, separation, height, visibility or polarity**. This does not mean that every physical maximum belongs to North; the correspondence applies when the wheel's own fourfold geometry places the relevant maximum at its N anchor.

## West — Water — Blue

West is transformation, crisis and encounter with the unknown.

Its primary principle is **Mystery**.

West represents reality beyond our descriptions of it: inexhaustible, fundamentally not reducible to any single model. Whereas East organizes a world through conscious concepts, West confronts the person with what exceeds those concepts.

Water expresses this through instability, movement, lived sensation, changing circumstances, dreams, plots, events and continual transformation.

Psychologically, West corresponds to the **subconscious** and, in the simple archetypal formulation, to the **Soul**. It includes dream, imaginal and magical modes of reality, not because any single esoteric description is treated as final, but because West continually undermines final descriptions.

This is the **crisis phase** of development. Crisis is not an error in the process; it is a necessary point where the intended transformation becomes real but remains unstable.

When a process reaches West, a new state may begin to appear directly in lived reality. The person can feel that something is already happening, but the state is not yet consolidated. West therefore accumulates **energy**: the new condition must gather enough energy to become stable. If it does not, the process can fall back and must approach the transformation again.

West also carries the danger of attachment to **power**. The unknown can become an inexhaustible source of force, altered experience or magical possibility, and this itself can become a stopping point rather than a passage.

## South — Creative Fire — Yellow / Gold

South is completion, synthesis, knowledge and the opening of a new evolutionary turn.

The Chakruna Fire is specifically **creative, generative and life-giving**, not destructive fire. Its yellow/golden color expresses solar, luminous and life-bearing flame.

South stabilizes what was unstable in West. A temporary event becomes a capacity. A lived transformation becomes **knowledge**.

This knowledge can be silent, embodied and operational. It does not need conceptual explanation in order to function. The body may know what to do before the mind can describe how it does it.

This is why Chakruna distinguishes strongly between:

- **understanding** — primarily Eastern, conceptual, describable;
- **knowledge** — Southern, integrated, embodied, stable.

Psychologically, South corresponds to the deepest **unconscious**, but also to the possibility that the unconscious becomes integrated and opens into **superconsciousness**. In the simple archetypal formulation, South corresponds to the **Divine Spark**.

South therefore represents not only an ending but also the source of the next cycle.

### Unity, binding and gravity

A major Southern correspondence is **binding**: the force by which many parts are held together as one system.

In Chakruna, the Southern Fire can therefore be understood as the hidden unity of a system — an unmanifest or unconscious wholeness that keeps differentiated parts related to one another. This is one reason South is associated simultaneously with completion, integration, the Divine Spark and the seed.

At the physical level, **gravity** is an important Chakruna correspondence to this Southern quality. Gravity gathers, binds and maintains large-scale material systems: bodies, planets, stars and orbital structures. The symbolic correspondence is not that every gravitational phenomenon is "Fire" in a scientific classification, but that gravitational attraction visibly manifests a principle Chakruna associates with South: **many parts held in one whole**.

This gives a useful North/South polarity:

```text
South -> concentration / binding / unity / inward gathering
North -> extension / polarity / separation / axial unfolding
```

East and West are the transitions between these poles:

```text
South -> East -> North
concentration -> manifestation -> expansion / maximum unfolding

North -> West -> South
maximum unfolding -> transformation -> reintegration
```

In pairwise distance cycles this correspondence can become physically concrete. For example, on the Sun–Earth Bind wheel:

- South is minimum Sun–Earth distance, approximately perihelion;
- North is maximum Sun–Earth distance, approximately aphelion.

Within that pairwise cycle, solar gravitational acceleration is larger at the smaller distance and smaller at the larger distance because it varies approximately as `1 / r²`.

The same pattern applies to the Earth–Moon Bind wheel:

- South is minimum Earth–Moon distance, approximately perigee;
- North is maximum Earth–Moon distance, approximately apogee.

These astronomical facts are physical; the interpretation of minimum-distance binding as a Southern Chakruna quality is the symbolic layer.

### Seed / egg archetype

A central Southern image is the **seed** or **cosmic egg**.

The seed appears small, inert, hidden and even lifeless, yet it contains the whole future organism in latent form. The completed cycle has been condensed into a compact source from which a new manifestation can grow.

This is the paradox of South:

- it is the end of one process;
- it is simultaneously the hidden beginning of the next;
- it contains the whole pattern, but not yet in manifested form.

The cosmic egg is a related mythic image: undifferentiated totality precedes the unfolding of a world. The Pangu cosmic-egg myth can be used as one comparative example of this archetype, but Chakruna does not claim that its fourfold map is historically derived from that myth.

The transition into the next cycle is therefore:

```text
South -> Seed / Egg / Divine Spark / Latent Whole
East  -> Sprout / First Manifestation
North -> Axis / Tree / Ascent / Growth
West  -> Mystery / Crisis / Transformation
South -> Mature Knowledge condensed into Seed again
```

## Consciousness path

The four directions can also be read as a path through layers of consciousness:

```text
East  -> conscious mind / ego
North -> awareness / spirit
West  -> subconscious / soul
South -> unconscious integrated toward superconsciousness / Divine Spark
```

In this reading, many people remain primarily identified with the Eastern human world of ordinary descriptions and established social reality.

Movement toward North begins when awareness separates from ordinary automatic life and enters a path of discipline, attention and spiritual effort. The danger here is becoming attached to **clarity** itself.

Movement toward West means entering deeply into the subconscious, dream, mystery and alternative descriptions of reality. The Western world can reveal extraordinary power because it is not exhausted by the conscious description of the world. The danger here is becoming attached to **power** and to magical or esoteric descriptions as if they were final.

To pass through West is to preserve Mystery itself as fundamental: however reality is described, it remains inexhaustible.

South is reached when the differentiated aspects are integrated rather than merely opposed. In Chakruna language, the unconscious can then become the basis of **superconsciousness**: the hidden unity becomes consciously lived without being reduced to an intellectual description.

## Process example: learning to ride a bicycle

The bicycle example is a practical model of the four phases.

**East / Earth.** A person wants to learn to ride. There is an image, desire and plan, but no embodied skill.

**North / Air.** Practice begins. The person must concentrate, repeat attempts, fall, recover and overcome resistance. Awareness, effort and discipline are required.

**West / Water.** The person suddenly begins to ride for short periods. The new reality is directly experienced, but it remains unstable. The rider may enter and lose the state repeatedly. Energy of the new capacity is accumulating.

**South / Fire.** Eventually the body simply knows how to ride. The skill has become stable and can operate without verbal calculation. From this silent knowledge, the person can later create new explanations, instructions or teaching — which become new Eastern forms.

## Chakruna in the existing wheel geometry

The current Chrono Compass wheel architecture already uses a common four-anchor geometry:

```text
E -> N -> W -> S -> E_next
```

The source order is visible in `src/lib/wheel/spokes.ts`, and several wheel types assign concrete astronomical events to the same E/N/W/S anchors. Chakruna can read those physical cycles through the deeper process order:

```text
S -> E -> N -> W -> S
```

This does **not** mean that the astronomy was created from Chakruna symbolism. The astronomy remains independently computed. Chakruna is a semantic reading of recurring fourfold structures already present in the physical cycles.

### Synod / lunar phases

In `src/lib/catalog/wheels/synod.ts`:

```text
S -> conjunction / new phase
E -> first quarter
N -> opposition / full phase
W -> last quarter
S -> conjunction / new phase
```

For the Earth–Moon lunar synodic cycle this gives:

**South / New Moon.** The Moon is at conjunction with the Sun as seen from Earth and is minimally illuminated from the terrestrial viewpoint. Chakruna reads this as latent wholeness: the visible form has withdrawn and the next cycle is contained as a seed.

**East / First Quarter.** The new cycle has become visibly established. The sprout is no longer merely latent.

**North / Full Moon.** Sun and Moon are opposite in the terrestrial sky. The lunar disk is maximally illuminated. Chakruna reads this as maximum unfolding, visibility, polarity and clarity.

**West / Last Quarter.** The achieved fullness is dissolving. The cycle passes through transformation toward renewed invisibility and reintegration.

The gravitational geometry adds another layer. At both new and full Moon, the Sun, Earth and Moon are approximately aligned, so the solar and lunar **tidal** effects reinforce one another and produce spring-tide conditions. The physical distinction is geometry:

- at new Moon, Sun and Moon lie approximately in the same terrestrial sky direction;
- at full Moon, they lie approximately in opposite terrestrial sky directions with Earth between them.

Chakruna can therefore read new Moon as **directional concentration** and full Moon as **axial polarity / extension**.

This should not be described as a large literal compression of a human body at new Moon or stretching at full Moon. For a local observer, ordinary solar and lunar attraction is largely shared with Earth; the physically relevant differential effect is the tidal gradient. The Chakruna reading concerns geometry and symbolic correspondence, not a claim of a large extra body force.

### Daily horizon cycle

In `src/lib/catalog/wheels/horizon.ts`:

```text
S -> minimum altitude / bottom culmination
E -> rising horizon
N -> maximum altitude / top culmination
W -> setting horizon
S -> minimum altitude
```

This is one of the clearest physical analogies to the Chakruna cycle:

- South: hidden depth, lower culmination, latent return;
- East: appearance above the horizon, emergence;
- North: greatest height and visibility;
- West: disappearance through the horizon, transition into the unknown;
- South: deepest hidden phase before the next rising.

For the Sun, local midnight is approximately the Southern side of the daily cycle: the Sun is below the observer, roughly in the direction through Earth rather than above the horizon. Chakruna can read this as an inward, centered or concentrated geometry.

Physically, a person does not simply gain the Sun's full gravitational acceleration as extra weight at midnight because Earth and observer are both accelerating in the solar field. Again, the scientifically meaningful local difference is tidal, while the directional alignment remains a valid geometric fact.

### Season wheel

In `src/lib/catalog/wheels/season.ts`:

```text
S -> south solstice
E -> east equinox
N -> north solstice
W -> west equinox
S -> south solstice
```

For Sun–Earth geometry:

- South is the state where Earth's north axis is maximally directed away from the Sun;
- East is the neutral crossing toward greater northward solar activity;
- North is the state where Earth's north axis is maximally directed toward the Sun;
- West is the complementary neutral crossing back toward the Southern side.

For Earth these correspond approximately to the December solstice, March equinox, June solstice and September equinox respectively. Seasonal words such as "winter" and "summer" depend on hemisphere and should not be treated as universal Chakruna labels.

The Chakruna reading is geometric:

- South: concealed or concentrated turning point from which the next ascent begins;
- East: emergence and renewed manifestation;
- North: maximum axial exposure and extension;
- West: transition away from the maximum into transformation;
- South: completion and return.

### Bind / distance cycles

In `src/lib/catalog/wheels/bind.ts`:

```text
S -> minimum distance
E -> midpoint crossing while distance rises
N -> maximum distance
W -> midpoint crossing while distance falls
S -> minimum distance
```

This wheel gives the clearest direct physical bridge to the Chakruna gravity correspondence.

For Sun–Earth:

```text
South ~ perihelion
North ~ aphelion
```

For Earth–Moon:

```text
South ~ perigee
North ~ apogee
```

The exact events are computed as local distance extrema in the wheel; the familiar orbital names describe the corresponding cases.

Chakruna reads the South side as **binding / concentration / closest union** and the North side as **maximum extension / separation**.

### Nodal cycles

In `src/lib/catalog/wheels/nodal.ts`:

```text
S -> minimum latitude / south nadir
E -> ascending node
N -> maximum latitude / north apex
W -> descending node
S -> minimum latitude
```

This produces another expression of the same fourfold geometry:

- South: deepest negative extremum;
- East: upward crossing into manifestation on the positive side;
- North: maximum positive extension;
- West: downward crossing and transition;
- South: return to the deepest negative extremum.

Here the Chakruna pattern appears not as distance or illumination but as a wave relative to a reference plane.

## A common wheel grammar

The wheel examples suggest a reusable Chakruna grammar:

| Direction | Recurrent wheel geometry | Chakruna reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| South | conjunction, minimum distance, minimum altitude, negative extremum, south-solstice turning point | concentration, latent whole, seed, binding, completion, hidden source |
| East | rising crossing, first quarter, ascending node, neutral crossing toward growth | manifestation, sprout, initiation, first stable form |
| North | opposition, maximum distance, maximum altitude, positive extremum, north solstice | extension, polarity, axis, visibility, clarity, maximum unfolding |
| West | setting crossing, last quarter, descending node, neutral crossing toward South | transformation, crisis, Mystery, dissolution of achieved form |

This table is **not a rule that every physical maximum or minimum in nature must belong to a particular direction**. It summarizes how the current Chrono Compass wheels already place their four principal anchors, and how Chakruna interprets those placements.

The deeper proposed principle is that different natural processes can be different projections of one fourfold pattern:

```text
South -> hidden integrated whole
East  -> first manifestation
North -> maximum differentiation / extension
West  -> transformative return through Mystery
South -> reintegrated whole
```

## Relation to the Epoch Calendar

Chrono Compass currently uses the fourfold sequence in the Epoch Calendar, including the four seven-day weeks of each 28-day Month:

```text
Week 1 -> East  -> Red   -> Earth -> Initiation
Week 2 -> North -> White -> Air   -> Purification / Ascent
Week 3 -> West  -> Blue  -> Water -> Transformation / Crisis
Week 4 -> South -> Gold  -> Fire  -> Completion / Knowledge / Evolution
```

This mapping is a Chakruna interpretive layer. Calendar arithmetic does not depend on accepting or displaying it.

Future UI should therefore be able to expose Chakruna symbolism as optional explanatory content without conflating it with structural calendar calculation, Dreamspell meanings, traditional Maya meanings, or astronomical data.
