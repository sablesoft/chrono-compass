Chrono Compass: calendars and astronomical cycles

Chrono Compass is an experimental visual time instrument for exploring calendars and interacting cycles. Its Epoch Calendar uses thirteen 28-day months and free days between years. Compare Gregorian dates and explore lunar phases, seasons and other astronomical cycles in the Wheels Dashboard. The application works offline after installation.
